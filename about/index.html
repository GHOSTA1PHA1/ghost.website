Ghost / About

I’m a hands-on builder who fixes the ugly stuff behind the scenes.

I work where code, systems, servers, and real-world troubleshooting crash into each other. My lane is taking messy technical problems, finding the actual cause, and turning them into something stable, useful, and easier to manage.

What makes me different

I don’t just stare at the front-end and pretend the rest of the system is magic. I can move from a broken UI to a server log, from a GitHub Pages folder issue to a Linux problem, and from a game server bug to the config that started the fire.

Practical first. Clean second. Flashy only when it earns its keep.

My working style

  • I look for the real cause, not just the loudest symptom.
  • I prefer simple, maintainable setups over fragile wizardry.
  • I document structure so projects do not become mystery boxes.
  • I keep pushing until the broken thing becomes the working thing.
